## 1. What Tusab is

Tusab is a local connector (MCP server) that works inside AI tools you choose, such as Claude Desktop. It lets you:

- extract YouTube video transcripts and keep documents, texts, web pages and public-source results in a folder on your computer;
- give your AI tool the excerpts from that base, with the source indicated;
- keep study materials produced by the AI and organize spaced repetition.

**Tusab is not an AI service.** The one that talks, summarizes and generates content is the AI tool you chose, under its own terms and costs.
